This page explains what information northernnationalmusic.com collects, why, and who helps us handle it. If you have questions, email [contact@northernnational.com](mailto:contact@northernnational.com).

## What we collect

**When you browse the site.** We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site: pages viewed, roughly where visitors are, the device and browser used, and actions like viewing merch or clicking play. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. It does not give us your name or email address.

**When you sign up for show alerts.** We collect your email address so we can email you about shows and releases. The list is run by Mailchimp.

**When you buy merch.** Checkout is handled by Stripe, which collects your payment details, email, phone number and shipping address. We never see or store your full card number. To print and ship your order we pass your name, shipping address, email and the items you bought to Printful.

## How we use it

- To run the site and see what's working.
- To send the show and release emails you asked for.
- To process, print, ship and support your orders.

We don't sell your information.

## Who helps us

- **Google Analytics** for site statistics.
- **Mailchimp** for the email list.
- **Stripe** for payments.
- **Printful** for printing and shipping merch.
- **Vercel** for hosting the site.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy that covers how it handles data.

## Your choices

- **Emails:** every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **Analytics:** you can block cookies in your browser or use an ad or tracker blocker.
- **Your data:** email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

## Changes

If we change this policy we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
